RootIQ UC18 — Company Policy Knowledge
 Families
 Policy-family blueprint for the RAG knowledge layer
Important: These are company-specific policy families designed to organize evidence from the TWCS customer-support dataset.
They are not claims that every listed rule is an official current company policy. TWCS-derived rules must be stored as
historical_inference with authoritativeness = inferred unless an authoritative company policy document is later supplied.
Recommended RAG structure: company → policy family → rule → conditions → thresholds/time constraints → product → region →
evidence → confidence → provenance.

How these policy families should enter RootIQ RAG
1. Evidence first: Extract rules only from actual historical evidence. Do not turn complaints such as 'my refund is late' into policies.
2. Company scope: Attach each candidate to the support handle/company that produced the evidence.
3. Product and region: Preserve product/region only when reliably supported by source data. Otherwise use null/unknown rather
than guessing.
4. Contradictions: If historical evidence contains conflicting rules (for example 7 days versus 14 days), preserve both candidates and
mark the group as conflicting_evidence.
5. Policy versus runbook: Policies describe what is allowed/required/expected. Runbooks describe what support should do.
6. Trust: Historical TWCS rules should have lower trust than future authoritative company policy/help-center documents.
Suggested policy document fields
document_id, document_type = policy, company, policy_type, category, subcategory, product, region, rule, conditions, thresholds,
time_constraints, exceptions, supporting_evidence, source_conversation_ids, source_timestamps, evidence_count,
distinct_source_count, confidence, provenance, authoritativeness, model_version, created_at.
